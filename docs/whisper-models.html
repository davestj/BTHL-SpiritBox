Voice Models How BTHL-SpiritBox turns audio into text

Transcription runs entirely on your computer — nothing is uploaded, and you don't need an internet connection during a session. The app uses a local speech model to turn the swept radio audio (and, in Interactive mode, your spoken questions) into readable text.

Loading a model

A recommended model is installed with the app and ready to use. To choose a different one:

  1. Open Whisper → Load Model… (or the Load Whisper Model… button in the Transcriptions panel).
  2. Pick a model file — the picker opens in the app's models folder automatically.
  3. The button reads Model Loaded once it's ready, and transcription begins.

Choosing the right model

Bigger models are more accurate but need more computer power. Most investigators do well with the default; step up if you want cleaner transcriptions and your machine keeps up.

ModelAccuracySpeedGood for
Base (English)GoodFastDefault — responsive sessions on most machines

Large TurboHighestSlowerBest accuracy; also handles other languages
Tip. Start with the default Base (English) model. If transcriptions feel rough, switch to Small or Medium from the Whisper menu — no restart needed.

Adding more models

Additional model files can be placed in the app's models folder and they'll appear in the Load Model picker. If you're not sure, the included model is all you need to start.

Privacy. All transcription happens locally on your device. Your audio and transcripts never leave your computer.
