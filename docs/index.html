BTHL-SpiritBox Documentation Paranormal Investigation Command Center — SDR Spirit Box · EMF Correlation · AI Voice Detection

BTHL-SpiritBox sweeps radio frequencies, demodulates the audio in real time, and uses local AI (whisper.cpp) to transcribe both the responses it captures and — when you enable the microphone — your own spoken questions. EMF readings from a GQ EMF-390 are correlated against voice events, and everything is archived to a reviewable session.
