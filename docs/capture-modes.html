Capture Modes Three one-click ways to run a session

The capture mode controls whether your microphone is recorded and how the transcript is framed. Pick one from the mode selector in the Transcriptions panel. Only one mode is active at a time, and the choice is saved into the session archive.

ModeMicrophoneRadio sweepTranscriptBest for
Interactive ONON Investigator questions + radio responses (full Q&A) Asking questions and listening for replies
Standalone OFFON Responses only Hands-off capture; you don't want your voice recorded
Passive Listen OFFON (continuous) Responses only, tagged spontaneous Leaving the box running in case the other side initiates

Interactive

The microphone is captured, run through voice-activity detection, and transcribed by the same Whisper model as the radio audio. Your questions appear as INVESTIGATOR: lines and the radio-band responses appear as RESPONSE @ <freq> lines, interleaved by time. This is the mode to use when you're actively conducting a Q&A session.

Privacy. Investigator audio is only ever used for transcription — it is never correlated with EMF spikes, so your own questions can't be mislabeled as a spirit response.

Standalone

The microphone is off. Only the swept radio audio is transcribed. Use this when you want a clean record of responses without your own voice in the file, or when you're observing rather than asking.

Passive Listen

Also microphone-off, but framed for an open-ended "listening watch" — you're not driving a Q&A, you're leaving the box sweeping in case something comes through on its own. Responses are recorded and tagged as spontaneous in the session metadata so you can tell a passive watch apart from an interactive session later.

Switching mid-session is fine. The mode change is reflected immediately in the status bar (MODE: …) and recorded, so your archive shows exactly how each part of the session was captured.
