About BTHL-SpiritBox

BTHL-SpiritBox is a paranormal investigation command center: an SDR frequency sweeper, real-time audio demodulator, local AI voice-detection pipeline (whisper.cpp), GQ EMF-390 correlation engine, and multi-sensor session archive. It is a companion to the BTHL-SpectraSentry Tactical Skanner.

Author: David St John (davestj) · davestj@gmail.com

Organization: Beyond The Horizon Labs

Version: 1.0.0

Project: github.com/davestj

How it works

BTHL-SpiritBox brings several investigation tools together into one console:

  • Radio sweep — a software-defined radio rapidly scans frequencies to create the spirit-box audio stream.
  • On-device AI transcription — speech is turned into text locally, with no cloud connection.
  • EMF correlation — readings from a GQ EMF-390 are matched against voice events in time.
  • Session archive — audio, transcripts, and EMF data are saved together for later review.
Real data only. Every reading shown and recorded comes from your actual hardware or genuine analysis of it. When a device isn't connected, the app simply shows nothing for it — it never invents or simulates readings.
