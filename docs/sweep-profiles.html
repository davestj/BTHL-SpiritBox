Sweep Profiles

A sweep profile defines which frequency bands the SDR scans, the step size, dwell time, and the demodulation mode. Pick one from the profile dropdown in the control panel.

Ghost Sweep (device-wide)

The Ghost Sweep profile stretches the radio across the widest range your hardware supports — far beyond the broadcast bands. It sweeps quickly and in a randomized order for the classic rapid spirit-box feel. The app automatically clips it to your tuner's real range (shown in Devices → Device Capabilities): a wideband tuner unlocks a huge span, while a limited tuner simply scans what it can reach — no wasted time on unreachable frequencies.

Best for open-ended "passive listen" watches where you want maximum spectrum coverage rather than a specific band.
E4000 tuners and AM. If your dongle uses the Elonics E4000 tuner (~52 MHz floor), any profile that includes the AM band will throw PLL not locked on every AM step and skip it. On an E4000, prefer FM Broadcast or VHF Low Band. See Hardware Setup.

Dwell time and pacing

Dwell time is how long the SDR rests on each frequency before stepping. Shorter dwell = faster, choppier sweep; longer dwell = slower, with more audio per channel. The default (~30 ms) suits most sessions. Adjust with the dwell slider, and remember to leave a 5–10 second listening window after each question regardless of dwell (see the Session Templates).

Which profile for EVP?

FM Broadcast is the most reliable starting point — it sweeps a dense band of stations on any tuner, producing the rapid fragmentary audio the technique relies on. Experiment with AM (on a capable tuner) and VHF once you're comfortable.
