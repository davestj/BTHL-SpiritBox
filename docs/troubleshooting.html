Troubleshooting

The sweep skips frequencies / "PLL not locked"

Your SDR's tuner can't reach those frequencies — most commonly the AM band on dongles with an E4000 tuner. BTHL-SpiritBox detects this and automatically skips the unreachable parts of the band. Check the Device Capabilities panel (Devices → Device Capabilities) to see your tuner's real range, and pick an FM or VHF profile if AM isn't supported.

No SDR found

  • Make sure the dongle is firmly seated in a USB port (try a different port, not a hub).
  • Use Devices → Refresh SDR Devices.
  • Close any other radio app that might be using the dongle — only one app can use it at a time.

No audio

  • Confirm a sweep is running — the frequency readout should be changing.
  • Check the volume slider in the app and your computer's output device.

My questions aren't being transcribed

  • Switch to Interactive mode — the other modes intentionally leave the microphone off.
  • Make sure a voice model is loaded (Whisper → Load Model…).
  • macOS: allow microphone access when prompted. To change it later, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and enable BTHL-SpiritBox.

"Another instance is already running"

Only one copy can run at a time because the SDR can't be shared. Close the existing BTHL-SpiritBox window, then reopen.

The transcript is empty when I save

Nothing has been transcribed yet. Load a voice model, run a sweep, and — to capture your own questions — switch to Interactive mode. The app tells you when there's nothing to save.

EMF readings aren't showing

  • Confirm the GQ EMF-390 is connected by USB and powered on.
  • Use Devices → Refresh EMF Ports, then reconnect.
  • The Device Capabilities panel shows whether the unit was detected.
Still stuck? Quit and relaunch the app, and reconnect your hardware. Most issues clear with a fresh start and a solid USB connection.
