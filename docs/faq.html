Frequently Asked Questions

What is a "spirit box"?

A spirit box (or ghost box) rapidly sweeps radio frequencies to create a continuous stream of fragmentary audio. The paranormal-investigation premise is that a presence can shape that stream into words — Electronic Voice Phenomena (EVP). BTHL-SpiritBox implements this with a software-defined radio and adds AI transcription, EMF correlation, and full session archival.

Is this scientifically proven?

No. The spirit-box technique is not scientifically validated, and sweep audio is noise by design — the human mind readily finds words in randomness (pareidolia). This tool is built for investigators and enthusiasts who want a rigorous, reviewable record: timestamps, transcripts, WAV audio, and EMF correlation, so claims can be examined rather than taken on faith. Stay skeptical.

Does my audio leave the machine?

No. Transcription uses whisper.cpp locally. Nothing is uploaded.

Why does it capture my voice?

Only in Interactive mode, so your questions appear in the transcript alongside responses — turning a session into a readable Q&A. Standalone and Passive Listen modes never record the mic. Your voice is never correlated with EMF as a "response."

Is the data real, or is any of it simulated?

Everything is real. All readings come from your connected hardware (SDR, EMF meter, microphone) or genuine analysis of those signals. If a device isn't connected, the app shows nothing for it — it never fabricates, randomizes, or simulates readings.

What hardware do I actually need?

At minimum an RTL-SDR dongle. A GQ EMF-390 and a microphone are optional but unlock EMF correlation and question-capture respectively. See Hardware Setup.

Why can't I scan the AM band?

If your tuner is an Elonics E4000, it physically can't tune below ~52 MHz. Use FM or VHF. See Sweep Profiles.

Which Whisper model should I use?

base.en to start, small.en for better accuracy. See Whisper Models.

How do I save a session?

Arm recording for a full WAV + JSON archive, and/or use File → Save Transcript… for a text export at any time.
