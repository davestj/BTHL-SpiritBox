BTHL-SpiritBox

Paranormal Investigation Command Center — an SDR spirit box with on-device AI voice transcription, EMF correlation, and full session archival.

This installer places the app in your personal Applications folder (~/Applications/BTHL/BTHL-SpiritBox) — no administrator password required — along with the base speech model. Larger models download on demand from inside the app.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 12 or later. An RTL-SDR is required for the radio sweep; a GQ EMF-390 and microphone are optional.
